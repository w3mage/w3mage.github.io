Privacy Policy – Meezan Raqba Calculator

Effective date: 3 November 2026
App: Meezan Raqba Calculator (Meezan Raqba) · Developer: w3mage

1. Summary

Meezan Raqba Calculator adds up land areas written in kanal, marla and square feet. The developer does not collect your personal information. The areas you enter and your settings stay on your phone. The calculator works without an internet connection.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on a server, or receive any personal information from the app. There is no account, sign-up or login. The app does not include analytics or crash-reporting tools.

3. Data stored on your device

To work as expected, the app saves the following only on your own device: the list of areas you entered, your language choice, your feet-per-marla setting, keypad size, and key-vibration setting. This data never leaves your phone through the app. You can remove the list at any time with the Clear all button, or remove all app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

4. Permissions

Versions of the app without ads do not need internet access and request no special permissions. Versions that show ads also use internet access, network state and the Android advertising ID so that ads can be loaded (see section 5).

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Some versions of the app may show ads served by Google AdMob. If the version you installed shows no ads, this section does not apply to you.

When ads are shown, Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, general device and app information, and how you interact with ads, in order to serve, measure and protect ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before ads are requested. For details see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (usually under Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads; the exact path depends on your phone).
  • You can use the calculator without any account.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time, which removes the data stored by the app on your device.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone. The only third party that may receive data is the advertising provider described in section 5, in versions of the app that show ads.

8. Children

The app is a general-purpose calculator and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

The app has no servers of its own. Data saved by the app is kept in the app's private storage area on your phone, protected by Android's normal app isolation.

10. Disclaimer

This is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government department or land-records authority. Results are provided for convenience; please verify important figures against official records.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (for example when ads or other services are added), this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Please check this page from time to time.
